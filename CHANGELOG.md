# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-09

First release.

- Classic rules: safe first click with a guaranteed opening, flood fill, chording, flags and question marks
- Beginner, Intermediate, Expert and custom boards (up to 50 × 30)
- Light and dark themes, switchable live
- Animations: pressed cells, reveal ripple, loss shake and mine cascade, win confetti
- Full keyboard play
- Statistics per difficulty and top-5 best times
- Settings remembered between launches
- Optional synthesized sound effects
- JUnit 5 test suite and GitHub Actions CI
